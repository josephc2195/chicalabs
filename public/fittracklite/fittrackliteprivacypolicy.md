# FitTrackLite Privacy Policy

FitTrackLite keeps all of your information on your device. Chicas Apps does not collect, receive, store or share any of your personal data.

This policy explains what FitTrackLite stores on your iPhone, how it uses Apple Health, and the choices you have. It applies to the FitTrackLite iOS app, including its widget and Live Activity.

**Effective date:** November 20, 2026

## Information stored on your device

Everything you enter in FitTrackLite is saved only in the app's storage on your iPhone. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else.

| Type | Examples | Why it's stored |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Profile | Name, height, weight, body fat percentage, goal weight and body fat, any photo you choose to add | To personalize the app and show your progress toward your goals |
| Workouts | Routines, exercises, custom exercises, sets, reps, weights, rest times, run distances and times, workout history | To log your training and show your progress over time |
| Settings | Units, theme, default sets/reps/weight, routine order and folders | To remember your preferences |

If you back up your iPhone with iCloud or a computer, this data may be included in that backup. Apple handles backups under its own privacy policy, and we cannot access them.

## Apple Health (HealthKit)

FitTrackLite uses Apple Health only if you give permission, and only to support your workouts.

- **Read:** heart rate and walking/running distance, so you can see them live during a workout and review them afterward.
- **Write:** your completed workouts, so they count toward your Activity rings.

Health data is never used for advertising, marketing or data mining. It is never sold or shared with third parties, and FitTrackLite never stores it in iCloud.

You can change or turn off this access at any time in **Settings > Health > Data Access & Devices > FitTrackLite**. Workouts already saved to Apple Health stay there until you delete them in the Health app.

## Other device features

These features run entirely on your device and send nothing to us.

- **Notifications:** rest-timer and interval alerts are scheduled locally on your iPhone. We don't use push notifications or a notification server.
- **Widget and Live Activity:** your current workout can appear on your Lock Screen. The app shares this workout data with its widget through storage on your iPhone.
- **Apple Watch:** the app only checks whether a watch is paired. Heart rate from your watch reaches the app through Apple Health.
- **Photos:** if you add a photo, you choose it with Apple's photo picker. The app only gets the photo you select, never the rest of your library.

## What we don't do

- We don't collect or receive any of your data. FitTrackLite has no accounts and no servers.
- We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.
- We don't sell, rent or share your data with anyone.

If you choose to share diagnostics with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports. Apple controls that process, and those reports don't identify you.

## Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can edit or delete workouts, routines and profile details in the app at any time.

Deleting FitTrackLite from your iPhone permanently removes all of its data. Because we never have a copy, we cannot restore it. Workouts already saved to Apple Health stay in the Health app until you delete them there.

## Children's privacy

FitTrackLite is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If we change how FitTrackLite handles your information, for example by adding new features, we will update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
